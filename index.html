03 — what I've built

Selected projects

IoT · Embedded Systems · Mechatronics

Smart Automated Access & Ambient IoT Ecosystem

A touchless entry system paired with occupancy-aware lighting, built to cut unnecessary power draw and remove a high-touch entry point.

Manual doors and static lighting waste energy and create a high-touch surface at every entry — a bad combination for both efficiency and hygiene.

PIR and ultrasonic sensors detect approach and trigger a servo/stepper-driven door mechanism. An ESP32 handles sensing and connects to a cloud dashboard (Blynk/MQTT) for remote monitoring, while an LDR-based circuit dims ambient lighting based on daylight and occupancy.

Built a working enclosure that kept the sensor housing minimal and unobtrusive, so the mechanism reads as part of the space rather than a bolted-on gadget.

Tested detection range and false-trigger rate at varying approach angles, and validated that lighting response tracked ambient light changes without visible flicker or lag.

ESP32 / ESP8266 PIR + ultrasonic Servo/stepper drive MQTT / Blynk LDR dimming
OUTCOME
Touchless entry with adaptive lighting that only runs at full brightness when a space is actually occupied.
Robotics · Renewable Energy · Computer Vision

Autonomous Solar-Powered Agricultural Rover

A self-powered field rover that monitors soil and crop health without a person walking the field.

Manually scouting fields for soil and crop condition is slow, inconsistent, and doesn't scale.

A photovoltaic panel with a battery-management circuit powers the rover indefinitely in the field. Soil moisture and temperature/humidity sensors log ground conditions, an ESP32-CAM captures crop imagery, and ultrasonic/IR sensors handle obstacle avoidance.

Assembled the drive base, solar charging circuit, and sensor payload into a single rover chassis built to survive uneven outdoor terrain.

Ran field trials checking navigation reliability around obstacles and logging consistency of soil/crop readings over repeated passes.

Solar + battery mgmt ESP32-CAM Soil moisture / temp-humidity Ultrasonic / IR avoidance
OUTCOME
Continuous, autonomous soil and crop monitoring without manual field walks.
Industrial IoT · Data Analytics · Hardware Prototyping

Industrial Predictive Maintenance Vibration Analyzer

A compact device that reads machine vibration and flags a developing fault before it causes downtime.

Machinery failures often show up after the fact — by then, the downtime cost is already locked in.

An MPU6050 accelerometer captures real-time vibration, processed onboard with an FFT to isolate abnormal frequency components. Threshold breaches trigger an alert and can cut power to the monitored motor automatically.

Built the sensor-plus-microcontroller unit into a compact housing designed to clamp directly onto machinery.

Validated the FFT output against known-good vibration signatures and deliberately induced fault conditions to confirm the alert threshold triggered correctly.

MPU6050 accelerometer On-device FFT Threshold alerting Motor cutoff relay
OUTCOME
An early-warning layer that flags abnormal vibration before it becomes a failure.
Embedded Systems · Security · Logistics

Biometric & NFC-Enabled Industrial Asset Tracker

A secure checkout station for tools and equipment, replacing manual sign-out sheets with a logged, dual-authenticated system.

Manual tool checkout logs are easy to skip and hard to audit, and equipment condition often goes unrecorded.

Dual authentication combines an RFID/NFC reader with a fingerprint sensor (R307). Each transaction — user, timestamp, return status — logs to an OLED display and a cloud database, with optional GPS/geofencing for mobile assets.

Built the reader and display into a single station housing, sized to sit at a tool crib without getting in the way.

Ran repeated checkout/return cycles to confirm logging accuracy and verified the dual-auth flow rejected invalid attempts correctly.

RFID / NFC Fingerprint (R307) OLED display Cloud logging GPS / geofencing
OUTCOME
An auditable, dual-authenticated checkout log that replaced manual sign-out sheets.
Biomedical Engineering · Wearable Tech · Machine Learning

AI-Powered Ergonomic Posture Correction Wearable

A discreet wearable that catches poor posture during repetitive tasks and nudges the wearer to correct it in real time.

Repetitive assembly-line tasks encourage slouching that builds into real injury risk over time.

IMU sensors placed along the upper back and shoulder feed a real-time ML classifier (trained via Edge Impulse) that distinguishes correct posture from slouching, paired with a haptic motor for gentle feedback.

Built the sensor and feedback unit into a low-profile wearable form, prioritizing comfort over a session of continuous wear.

Tested classification accuracy across a range of posture positions and measured feedback latency from detected slouch to haptic cue.

IMU sensors Edge Impulse ML Haptic feedback Wearable form factor
OUTCOME
Real-time posture correction that intervenes before poor form becomes a habit.
